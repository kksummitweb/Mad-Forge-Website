FAQ

Answers before we start working together

Every question you might have about the process, what's included, timelines, and how we work — answered clearly below.

How long does a website project take?
Most projects are delivered within 2–4 weeks depending on scope and the speed of feedback rounds. Landing pages typically take less time, while full custom websites may take slightly longer. We'll give you a clear timeline before work begins.
Do you work with small businesses?
Yes — we primarily work with freelancers, local service businesses, consultants, and growing service brands. If you want a professional, trustworthy online presence that generates real leads, we're a great fit.
Can I request edits after the website is launched?
Absolutely. Post-launch support is available for updates, small improvements, and ongoing refinements as your business evolves. We want your website to keep improving over time, not become outdated.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes. Website refresh projects are available when your current site no longer reflects the quality of your business. We'll modernise the design, improve conversion elements, and clean up the structure while maintaining your brand identity.
What do I need to provide to get started?
To get started we typically need your business details, any existing branding (logo, colours), an idea of the style you want, and the content you'd like on the website. Don't worry if you're unsure — we'll guide you through everything during the discovery phase.
Do you provide content writing?
We primarily focus on design and build. You'll provide the core copy about your business, services, and value proposition. We can offer guidance on structure and messaging to ensure the content converts well on the page.
What platform or technology do you use?
We deliver clean, hand-coded HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no heavy page builders or unnecessary dependencies. This results in fast, lightweight, and professionally crafted websites you have full control over.
How do I get a quote?
Simply fill in the contact form with your project details. We'll review your request and reply within 24 hours with a recommended approach, scope, and pricing — no obligations.
Do you offer hosting or domain registration?
We focus on design and development. We can provide guidance on suitable hosting and domain options for your project, but registration and management are handled on your side so you maintain full ownership.
How many revisions are included?
A structured revision round is built into every project. We'll agree on the revision scope upfront so there's never any ambiguity. Our goal is to get it right — not to limit your input.
