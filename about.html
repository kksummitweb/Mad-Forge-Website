About Mad Forge

Professional websites for local businesses and startups

Mad Forge serves businesses in and around Madison, Wisconsin with clean, modern websites built for trust, speed, and long-term growth.

I started Mad Forge to give small businesses access to high-quality website design without agency complexity. Whether you are launching your first site or replacing an outdated one, the process is simple, transparent, and focused on results.

Every project is custom-built with responsive layouts, clear messaging structure, and practical SEO fundamentals. You get a site that looks premium and is easy to maintain.
